OUR STORY

About VeBlyss Global Pvt Ltd

Where Indian Craft Meets Global Standards

WHO WE ARE

At VeBlyss Global, we bring together India’s rich tradition, artisan heritage and contemporary lifestyles to deliver handpicked, high-quality products to global markets.

From artisan-made leather goods to eco-conscious lifestyle products, we thoughtfully curate products that reflect authenticity, craftsmanship and modern design.

Based in Bengaluru, India, we connect trusted artisans and producers with customers and partners worldwide. We specialise in curating, sourcing and exporting distinctive Indian products, with a strong focus on quality, responsible sourcing, reliability and long-term value.

OUR STORY

Built Around Quality, Authenticity & Purpose

VeBlyss was created to bridge the gap between authentic Indian craftsmanship and customers looking for products they can genuinely trust.

A bridge between skilled artisans, trusted producers and discerning customers.

We started VeBlyss after noticing how difficult it had become for consumers and businesses to find products that genuinely combine quality, authenticity, ethical sourcing, and value. Too often, handcrafted goods were overlooked, artisan communities struggled to access global markets, and consumers were left choosing between mass-produced products and products they could truly trust.

We wanted to create a brand that connects people with premium handcrafted fashion, lifestyle, and essential products that are responsibly sourced and thoughtfully made. Our goal is to help customers make more conscious purchasing decisions, enjoy healthier lifestyles, and experience products with a genuine story behind them.

What makes VeBlyss different is our belief that quality should never come at the expense of ethics. We work to bring together skilled artisans, trusted producers, and discerning customers through craftsmanship, transparency, sustainability, and long-term relationships.

At VeBlyss, we believe the best products are not only beautifully made and functional — they also support communities, preserve traditions, and contribute to a healthier and more meaningful way of living.

VISION & MISSION

What We Stand For

Our Vision

VeBlyss was founded with a simple vision: to bring ethically sourced, premium-quality handcrafted products and essential goods to customers who value authenticity, wellness, and responsible living. By connecting skilled artisans and trusted producers with global markets, we create products that deliver exceptional quality while supporting craftsmanship, sustainability, and healthier lifestyles.

Our Mission

Our mission is not simply to sell products; it is to share stories, preserve craftsmanship, and create opportunities. Every product we offer is carefully selected for its quality, authenticity, and the positive impact it can have on both the people who make it and the people who use it.
